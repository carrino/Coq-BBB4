=== Mp EXPONENTIAL-BOOT exemplar: 0RB1LC_1LA1RB_1LD0LA_1RD0RB ===
outer anchor family Mp@A.  boot(K=3..7) = 33, 61, 113, 213, 409  (ratio -> 2)
For contrast an AFFINE-boot machine boots in 4K+3 steps.

The question: what is the overflow phase doing that DOUBLES the walk to
the inner anchor?  Two candidates the measurements cannot separate:
  (a) a THREE-level nest, or
  (b) the inner counter really starts LOW (near 1) and runs all the way
      to 2^K-1, so the value 2^(K-1) that innerfam locks onto is reached
      exponentially late -- innerfam accepts a SUBSEQUENCE of a longer
      consecutive run as 'exactly 2^(K-1)..2^K-1'.
