WHAT EACH LIVE-CORE ROW'S CLASS ARMS NEED  (LADDER_PLAN 4k)
==========================================================

Measured from tools/ladder/core61_sweep.jsonl (valfam --kmax 9 --cap 150 over
the 61 core rows left after the first board; ~1.5 h, do not redo it).

Only the 21 binary / step-1 / one-phase rows are listed -- the others are
blocked before the arms (6 gray, 3 two-phase, 27 no family or none closed,
4 time cap).  For each, the shape of the two CLASS arms:

  interior  t^n ++ d :: rest -> 0^n ++ (d+1) :: rest,  n = off + stride*j
  fill      t^(1+off+stride*j) -> the fill law's target

"off" is the guaranteed copies materialised into s_pre; "stride" is the
block width.  A row needs BOTH arms.  With stride and offset on both, 15 of
21 have both; with stride on the interior only (the state at 4i) three do.
