    far side), W | V, the move of a nonempty and of an empty [x], its flags:

    stride %(sti)d, carries at %(n0c)d / %(stc)d, narrowings at
    %(n0n)d / %(stn)d, refills at %(n0r)d / %(str)d.  Every arm is a

  ReachL tm (if e <? fm_b FAM then negb (fm_left FAM)
             else if phnr_%(mid)s p then true else negb (fm_left FAM))
